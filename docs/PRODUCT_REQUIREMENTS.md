# Product Requirements Snapshot

## Child experience

- Child-facing instruction is primarily spoken Egyptian Arabic and clear English, short, patient, and age-appropriate.
- Core lessons do not assume independent reading or writing.
- Images and math scenes must reflect state actually rendered by the app.
- Touching an image is exploratory feedback, not a spoken answer or mastery event.
- Incorrect and unclear speech must not be marked correct; completion must be bounded and clear.
- Provide an easy path back to a parent for course changes.

## Parent experience

- Select grade/program/subject/unit/lesson and review progress and test answers.
- Provide a clear guardian gate before sensitive settings/actions.
- Explain local storage and AI audio transmission before use.

## Current verified offering

48 foundation lessons, 48 Connect Plus 1 lessons, and 44 Math Grade 1 Languages lessons. They are original activities aligned by topic, not a claim of complete reproduction of a specific school-book edition.

## Product gaps

Independent grading, multi-child accounts, privacy consent and retention, cross-device sync, parent gate, full offline mode, and publisher/edition curriculum validation are not yet complete.
