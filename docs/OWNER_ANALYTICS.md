Consistent read-only repeatable-read snapshot for synthetic accounts only. Active plan counts and active sources separately, student profiles, all-time order state and test-settled amount, current UTC month quota reservations (not provider consumption), trial redemption and discounted orders, non-revoked vs revoked device counts (not online presence). Integer minor totals as decimal strings, no personal data, no real revenue or cost values, no estimation without provider reconciliation. Existing owner permission and dedicated local staging DB. Real payment ledger, refunds, taxes, provider invoices, historical snapshots and actual revenue/cost reports deferred. No Paymob changes.
